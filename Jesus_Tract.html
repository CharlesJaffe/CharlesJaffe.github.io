Rotating electron configurations of He, Li, Be and B: frozen-shape orbits

Classical, nonrelativistic atoms with the nucleus at the origin (M = ∞). Each configuration is a shape of the electrons that can rotate rigidly about an axis: the planar rings are central configurations (CC); the others are balanced configurations (BC, Albouy and Chenciner 1998). Each animation holds the shape fixed while it turns about the rotation axis and its size oscillates. For the central configurations this is an exact solution, the Kepler-like homographic motion. For the balanced configurations the shape cannot stay fixed while the size changes, so the motion shown is the frozen-shape approximation; only uniform rigid rotation is exact for them.
