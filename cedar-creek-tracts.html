Cedar Creek tracts, 1750–1772

Henry Wetzel’s 455 acres, divided in 1772 between Philip Blay (Bley) and George Loughmiller, with the neighbouring tracts down to the mouth of Duck Run.

How the map was drawn

Each tract is plotted from the bearings and distances in its survey or deed, as written: magnetic bearings, distances in poles (one pole is 16½ feet). Tracts are joined where one survey calls a corner or line of another:

  • Thomas Butler and Andrew Vance share a corner and a line.
  • Aaron (James) Price’s survey runs 74 poles with Vance’s line from Vance’s corner C to the Cedar Creek crossing; Vance’s own survey puts the creek 126 poles from his corner B, which agrees.
  • Price’s survey then runs N 40 W 66 poles “with Richards’s line to his corner,” which is the first line of Richards’s 353 acres (Robert Rutherford’s surveys of 13 and 14 December 1752). The rest of the Duck Run block (Richards 400, 150, Marney) is joined through its own shared corners.

The map is a reconstruction, not a survey. The whole block floats: it is not tied to any fixed point on the ground, and positions are relative to one another only.

Placing Wetzel’s tract

No survey calls a line or corner that ties Henry Wetzel’s 455 acres to its neighbours, so the tract, and with it the Blay and Loughmiller parts, is placed by fit. The fit is close. Wetzel’s line E–A and Vance’s line D–C are both 274 poles, with bearings 4° apart. Laid together, with Wetzel’s corner A at Vance’s corner C and the tract turned 4°, Wetzel’s land overlaps nothing. Price’s line then runs along Wetzel’s A–B for 51 poles, and Wetzel’s corner E falls within 8 poles of Butler’s corner C.

One call remains unexplained. Wetzel’s survey describes corner C as in Thomas Butler’s line and corner D as a corner to Butler. In this position Wetzel’s land meets Butler’s only near corner E, and corner D lies about 110 poles from Butler’s corner C. On the map, a thin red line runs from Wetzel’s corner D to Butler’s corner C, close alongside Loughmiller’s lower edge.

Still open

  • Wetzel’s plat shows Capt. John Funk’s 341 acres adjoining to the north-east. That tract has not been placed.
  • This block has not yet been tied to the Hoggs Creek tracts (Princely, Secrist, Catley and their neighbours); no shared corner has been found.
  • Several tracts compute to areas that differ from their stated acreage: Vance 371 acres for 400, Marney 180 for 160, Richards 150 at 165, Richards 353 at 343. The full list is at the foot of the map.
